{{- if .Values.casdoor.enabled }}

The bundled identity provider is at {{ include "map.casdoorOrigin" . }} — serve that host over
HTTPS, which the platform requires of an issuer and a key set and does not
negotiate. Sign in as `admin` in the `built-in` organization, with the
casdoor.adminPassword you set; that account is a Casdoor GLOBAL administrator, so
treat the value as a bootstrap credential and change it here first.

Then create your operators in the `map` organization and put each in one of the
seeded groups — map/platform-admins, map/platform-devs, map/platform-read. Those
names are what identity.roleMap keys on, and a human in none of them authenticates
and holds no role, which every role-gated route answers 403.

The seed is re-applied on every restart (initDataNewOnly=false, which is what
lets it own the admin account's password), so admin-UI edits to the entities it
names — that organization, the map-console application, those three groups, that
account — do not survive a restart. Accounts you create are not named by the seed
and do survive. Change what the seed says in your values, not in the UI.
{{- end }}
