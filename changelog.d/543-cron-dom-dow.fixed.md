- **A deployment schedule that restricts both day-of-month and day-of-week is refused** ([#543](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/543)) — a create or update whose `schedule.expression` writes both day fields as something other than `*`, such as `0 0 1 * 1`, now answers 400 `invalid_request_error` "`schedule.expression` cannot restrict both day-of-month and day-of-week; use `*` for one", as the reference was recorded answering. It used to be stored and to fire on the POSIX union, the 1st of each month or any Monday. A schedule of that shape stored before this release still lists its upcoming runs and fires on the union; an update that leaves its `schedule` out keeps it, and one that resends it is refused until one day field is `*`.
