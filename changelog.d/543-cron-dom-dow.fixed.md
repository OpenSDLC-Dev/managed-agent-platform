- **A deployment schedule that restricts both day-of-month and day-of-week is refused** ([#543](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/543)) — a create or update whose `schedule.expression` writes both day fields as something other than `*` now answers 400 `invalid_request_error` "`schedule.expression` cannot restrict both day-of-month and day-of-week; use `*` for one", as the reference was recorded answering a create with `0 0 1 * 1`. A field counts as restricted whenever it is not written `*`, even `*/1` or `1-31`. Such a schedule used to be stored and to fire on the POSIX union, the 1st of each month or any Monday. One stored before this release still lists its upcoming runs and fires on the union. An update that leaves its `schedule` out keeps it. An update that resends it is refused until one day field is `*`.
