- **`POST /v1/sessions/{id}` now follows the session's status, as the reference was recorded doing** ([#312](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/312)) — a `running` session refuses a body that carries `agent` with `400 invalid_request_error` and the reference's sentence, even when the patch would change nothing. Its `title` and `metadata` still update. A `rescheduling` session accepts all four fields, as it did before. An unarchived `terminated` session refuses every update with `Cannot modify archived session`. A client that patched `agent.tools` or `agent.mcp_servers` during a turn must now send `user.interrupt` by itself, wait for `idle`, and then patch. [docs/DIVERGENCES.md](./docs/DIVERGENCES.md) records the evidence and the edges no recording sampled.
