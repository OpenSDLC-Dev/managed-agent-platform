- **`POST /v1/sessions/{id}` now follows the session's status, as the reference was recorded doing** ([#312](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/312)) — a `running` session refuses a body that carries `agent` with `400 invalid_request_error` and the reference's sentence, even when the patch would change nothing. Its `title` and `metadata` still update. A client that patched `agent.tools` or `agent.mcp_servers` during a turn must now send `user.interrupt` by itself, wait for `idle`, and then patch. An archived session now answers an update with `Cannot modify archived session`, where it used to name the session, so a client matching the old words must change. An unarchived `terminated` session gets the same refusal, though nothing here stores that status yet. [docs/DIVERGENCES.md](./docs/DIVERGENCES.md) records the evidence and the edges no recording sampled.
