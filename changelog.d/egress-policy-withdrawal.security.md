- **Egress gate now applies live policy updates at outbound admission and severs withdrawn active work** ([#1052](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/1052); [plan 70](docs/plan/70_security-hardening-vault-egress.md) slice 3) — `gate.Gate.Update` now replaces networking policy and credentials in place on one long-lived gate instead of leaving in-flight handlers on superseded gate instances. Plain-HTTP requests buffer raw bodies before loading the current policy/credential snapshot for substitution and outbound admission, and both plain-HTTP and CONNECT dials re-check the current policy immediately before opening sockets. On policy or credential withdrawal (including `401` session revocation), `Update` drops idle pooled connections, immediately closes active CONNECT tunnels whose destination or address floor is no longer permitted, and cancels active plain-HTTP requests whose destination, address floor, or substituted credentials were withdrawn, while leaving unaffected tunnels and requests running.
