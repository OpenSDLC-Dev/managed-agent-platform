- **The model gateway keeps a ledger and enforces each key's limits** (plan 59 slice 2) — every request a key is admitted for is recorded once it is over, even after its caller has left — though not one still running when a stopping replica's 30-second shutdown grace runs out: the model it asked for and the deployment that answered, its session, the status and error it was given, the tokens its upstream reported and their cost at the deployment's prices, its latency and a stream's time to first token. Each row is added to a per-day rollup that is kept indefinitely, while the rows themselves are deleted after `MODELGATEWAY_USAGE_RETENTION` (90 days by default). `GET /admin/v1/usage/daily` and `GET /admin/v1/usage/requests` read them. A key policy's RPM and TPM are enforced across every replica, answering 429 `rate_limit_error` with `retry-after`; TPM counts input, cache-write and output tokens but not cache reads, as Anthropic's limits do.
