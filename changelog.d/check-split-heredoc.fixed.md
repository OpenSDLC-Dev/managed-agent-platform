- **`make gcp-split-check` could be walked past with a separator character** ([#761](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/761)) — the guard ends a heredoc where Terraform does, at the first line that trimmed is the terminator, but Python's `str.strip()` calls four characters whitespace that Terraform reads as ordinary body text. A `U+001C` before the terminator word therefore ended the string for the guard and not for Terraform, and the body lines it then took for structure could bury a `google_kms_crypto_key` below brace depth 0, where neither of the guard's two rules looks. The braces balance again at end of file, so no refusal fired: the check printed `ok` over an `environment/` that owned the key, on a tree `terraform fmt -check -recursive` exits 0 on. Those four characters are now excluded from the terminator comparison rather than trimmed, and a regression case carries the bypass file.
