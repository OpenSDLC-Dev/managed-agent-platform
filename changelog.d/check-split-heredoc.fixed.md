- **`check_split.py` no longer ends a plain heredoc at an indented terminator** ([#758](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/758)) — the split guard matched a heredoc terminator on the trimmed line, but Terraform ends `<<EOT` only at a line that *is* the terminator; indentation is allowed for `<<-EOT` and `<<~EOT` alone. So an indented `EOT` closed the string for the guard while Terraform read on, and everything between was configuration to one and text to the other — a resource reported that no apply would ever create, or a brace count desynced around one. The opener's flavour is now carried alongside the terminator and compared exactly for the plain form, which is how `tools/kmsrole`'s reader has always read it; the two are mirrors again. Found while building that reader for #750.
