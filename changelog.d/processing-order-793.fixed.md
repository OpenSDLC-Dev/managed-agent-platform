- **A commit's events are written in the order they are processed, as the reference lists them** ([#793](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/793), [#539](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/539)) — A `user.message` or `user.define_outcome` that wakes a session now follows the running pair it causes, on the list and the stream, for a send, a redirect, a create's `initial_events` and a dream stage. An interrupt follows the results and outcome ends it writes and precedes its idle pair, and `processed_at` now says the same. A send's answers keep their place among its interrupts, one thread's in the order its calls are processed with a denial's result beside it; an answer still queued behind an earlier call comes last, unprocessed. A report, a `send_to_agent` that wakes a child, and an ending notice follow the target's running event, and input that wakes nothing comes last. The POST echo keeps the posted order. Rows already on the log keep their old order, and an older brain replica writes the old order during a rolling upgrade; a client that reads these events by position sees them reordered. A child's resume of an idle session keeps this platform's shape, registered as deliberate in docs/DIVERGENCES.md. Archiving a session now marks a live outcome `interrupted` whatever its primary is parked on; GET used to report it as still live.
