- **A commit's events are written in the order they are processed, as the reference lists them** ([#793](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/793), [#539](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/539)) — A `user.message` or `user.define_outcome` that wakes a session now follows the `session.status_running` and primary `session.thread_status_running` it causes, on the list and the stream, for a send, a redirect, a create's `initial_events` and a dream stage. An interrupt now follows the results it synthesizes and any outcome ends, and precedes its idle pair, so a one-send redirect reads: results, `user.interrupt`, idle pair, running pair, `user.message`. A child's report, a `send_to_agent` that wakes an idle child, and an ending notice that wakes a coordinator now follow the target's running event. The POST echo keeps the posted order, and answers still precede the pair they cause. Rows already on the log keep their old order, since the log is append-only and not backfilled, and an older brain replica writes the old order during a rolling upgrade. A client that reads these events by position sees them reordered. A child's resume of an idle session keeps this platform's shape, now registered as deliberate in docs/DIVERGENCES.md.
