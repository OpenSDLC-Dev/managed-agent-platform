- **A primary thread can no longer carry `archived_at`** ([#720](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/720)) — #713 stopped a session archive stamping its `archived_at` onto the primary thread, and v0.4.0 shipped that with a migration clearing the stamps already there. A new `CHECK` on `session_threads` now refuses the stamp on any primary row; a child's archive is unaffected. Its migration first clears any stamp already written. No release wrote the stamp, so upgrading from any release needs nothing. A deployment tracking `main` at a build between the session-thread resource (#436) and #713 should roll out a build carrying #713 first, or every session archive its old replicas perform fails on the constraint until they are replaced; on such a deployment a new controlplane, brain or executor can also fail to start until a retry applies the migration.
