- **A thread an answer moves is written in processing order, a denial's result after a resume** ([#793](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/793)) — When a `user.tool_confirmation`, `user.tool_result` or `user.custom_tool_result` moves a thread, the send now makes the move itself, where the answer is consumed and in receipt order with any `user.interrupt` beside it, so the session no longer folds idle on a gate the answer cleared and then runs again. A resume's running pair follows the answer and precedes what the resumed turn reads: on the primary thread, a denial's `agent.tool_result` and a `user.message` posted beside the answer, as every recorded primary denial lists them. A thread left waiting on another call re-idles right after the answer, ahead of input still pending. A child's denial result stays ahead of the child's running event, and a denial that leaves another call waiting still writes its result at once, where the reference holds it until the thread resumes (docs/DIVERGENCES.md). A replayed request now answers a turn's tool calls in their order, which OpenAI-compatible backends may require. Rows already on the log keep their old order.
