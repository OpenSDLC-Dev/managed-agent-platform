- **A thread an answer resumes is listed in processing order, a denial's result after the resume** ([#793](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/793)) — When a `user.tool_confirmation`, `user.tool_result` or `user.custom_tool_result` resumes a thread parked on `requires_action`, the send now writes the resume's running pair itself, after the answer and ahead of what the resumed turn consumes, instead of after everything the send wrote. On the primary thread a denial's `agent.tool_result` now follows `session.status_running` and `session.thread_status_running`, as every recorded primary denial lists it, and a `user.message` posted beside the answer follows them too, where it used to precede the resume. A child's denial result stays ahead of the child's running event, as the one recorded child denial lists it. A denial that leaves another call of its thread waiting still writes its result at once; the reference holds it until the thread resumes (docs/DIVERGENCES.md). Rows already on the log keep their old order.
