- **Environment keys are refused in the reference's words** ([#840](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/840)) — A revoked environment key now answers 401 `authentication_error` "OAuth access token has been revoked." wherever it is presented, management routes included; an expired or unknown key keeps `invalid environment key`, and a key both revoked and expired answers as revoked. A live key sent as a Bearer to a management route gets the reference's recorded refusal: a 401 "Authentication failed" on `GET /v1/agents`, a 403 `permission_error` on `GET /v1/skills` and `GET /v1/skills/{id}`, which the key no longer reads. A skill's versions, a version and its `/content`, all a worker reads, still serve it, and other management routes keep the missing-key 401. Only a Bearer carrying the `sk-map-env01-` prefix is looked up there, so a management key's request costs no extra query, and a grandfathered pre-0021 key is not recognised. A key still goes with its deleted environment, a 401 where the reference's work stats answer 404; [docs/DIVERGENCES.md](docs/DIVERGENCES.md) says why.
