- **On Kubernetes, a command SIGKILLed just past its deadline by something other than its watchdog is reported as timed out, on a loaded cluster too** ([#838](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/838)) — a command that had killed its in-pod watchdog and was then SIGKILLed less than 0.5 s past its deadline, by the node's OOM killer say, could come back as exit 137 with `TimedOut: false` when the cluster was busy. The only witness was the pre-deadline liveness probe, an exec into the pod that under load answered after the kill. The exec wrapper's run-time record from #832 now also reads `/proc/uptime` just after the watchdog's launch, and a SIGKILL that ends a run longer than the deadline less 50 ms, counted from there, is a timeout. That is the line the probe already drew, from a later starting point. The record only ever adds a timeout, and only one a punctual probe would also have given, unless the wrapper stalls between reaping the command and its last reading. The docker backend is unchanged.
