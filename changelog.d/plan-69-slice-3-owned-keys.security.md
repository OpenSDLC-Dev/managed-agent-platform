Developers list and manage only their owned rows; admins manage non-preset rows. Owner and human creator render separately, with list-only `can_manage`. A principal-first transaction fences issuance and updates against role withdrawal, logout and removal. Migration 0061 refuses new human-unowned INSERTs while preserving historical rows.
