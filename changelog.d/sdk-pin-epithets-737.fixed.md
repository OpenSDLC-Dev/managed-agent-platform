- **SDK claims that named the pin only as "the pinned SDK" now carry the tag they were read at** ([#737](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/737)) — after the next SDK bump such an epithet would have named a release nobody re-read the claim against, and `tools/sdkref` could not see it. In `docs/DIVERGENCES.md` and the Go comments, an epithet that a dated citation in the same entry already covers is dropped, and one that no citation covered gains a `checked against` or `absent at` citation. `tools/sdkref`'s shape rung now also treats "Stainless spec" like "OpenAPI spec" and "bundled spec", so naming the spec for its generator no longer escapes it. The epithet itself is not a rule: "pinned" here more often means a test holding a behaviour.
