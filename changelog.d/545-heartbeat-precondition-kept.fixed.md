- **A heartbeat that omits `expected_last_heartbeat` keeps its 400, by decision** ([#545](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/545)) — the reference accepts that form as a weaker claim: it claims an unclaimed item, doing the ack on the way, and extends an active lease with no check. This platform still answers it `400 invalid_request_error`, so every heartbeat stays a compare-and-swap against the beat the client last saw. The SDK's worker, and so the `ant` worker, sends the field on every beat and is not affected. A hand-written client must send `NO_HEARTBEAT` on its first beat and echo `last_heartbeat` after that. [docs/DIVERGENCES.md](./docs/DIVERGENCES.md) records the four reasons and what the reference was recorded doing.
