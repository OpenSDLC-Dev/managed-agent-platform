- **The memories list refuses a `path_prefix` that does not end with `/`** ([#549](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/549)) — `GET /v1/memory_stores/{id}/memories?path_prefix=/notes` now answers 400 `invalid_request_error` `path_prefix must be segment-aligned and end with /`, as the reference was recorded answering. It used to match the bytes, so `/notes` also listed the memories under `/notes-archive/`. Send `/notes/` instead. The list's order, and its answer to `depth=0` beside a prefix, still differ from the reference; #549 stays open for them.
