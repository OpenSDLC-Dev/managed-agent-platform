- **A past `expires_at` on a new `mcp_oauth` credential is refused** ([#589](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/589)) — `POST /v1/vaults/{id}/credentials` with an `mcp_oauth` credential whose `auth.expires_at` is not in the future now answers 400 `invalid_request_error` `auth.expires_at must be in the future.`, the reference's recorded words. It used to store the credential, which on its first dial then refreshed, or sent its stale token when it had no `refresh` block. A credential update still accepts a past `expires_at`: a stored expiry passes with time, and a client such as the console resends it with every save.
