- **The model gateway answers OpenAI's Responses API, statelessly** (plan 59 slice 6, [#898](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/pull/898)) — `POST /v1/responses`, also under `/openai`, converts the request to Anthropic Messages and serves it as `/v1/messages` is — routing, retry, fallback, thinking provenance, limits and the ledger, and the conversion to Chat Completions for an OpenAI-only credential — and converts the answer back, whole or streamed. A thinking block comes back as a `reasoning` item whose `encrypted_content` is the gateway's wrapped signature, so a client that sends the output back as input keeps the vendor's thinking through a tool loop. `reasoning.effort` asks for adaptive thinking at that effort, `none` turns thinking off. Nothing is stored: `previous_response_id`, `conversation`, `prompt` and the routes under `/v1/responses/` are refused with a reason. Only function tools are served. Every disposition is in [docs/DIVERGENCES.md](docs/DIVERGENCES.md).
