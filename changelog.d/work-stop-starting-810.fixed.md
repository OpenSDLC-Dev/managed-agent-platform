- **A graceful work stop of acked work goes `stopping`, and the worker's claim learns of it** ([#810](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/810)) — a graceful `POST …/work/{work_id}/stop` of `starting` work (acked, but no heartbeat yet) now answers `200` `stopping` instead of stopping the work outright, as the recorded reference service does. The worker's `NO_HEARTBEAT` claim on such work now answers `200` with `lease_extended: false`, `state: "stopping"` and `last_heartbeat: ""` instead of `412`, and changes nothing. The worker then force-stops the work, so its session is re-armed once. If no worker claims it, a poll finalizes it after its startup lease and the one-minute wind-down window. A sessions token now stays valid while its work is `stopping`, so a worker that learns of the stop late can still finish it; past the one-minute window it reaches only that work's heartbeat and stop. Every other failed claim still answers `412`, and a graceful stop of queued work still stops it outright. `last_heartbeat` keeps its format whenever a heartbeat exists.
