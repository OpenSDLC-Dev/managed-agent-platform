- **A repeat work ack keeps its 200, by decision** ([#541](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/541)) — the reference refused an ack of an active item with `409 invalid_request_error`, and its message says it refuses any item that is no longer queued. This platform still answers such an ack 200 and leaves the item's state as it is. So when the SDK client retries an ack whose response was lost, the retry succeeds, and the SDK's poller does not force-stop the item. A client written for the reference's 409 still works. [docs/DIVERGENCES.md](./docs/DIVERGENCES.md) records the three reasons and what the reference was recorded doing.
