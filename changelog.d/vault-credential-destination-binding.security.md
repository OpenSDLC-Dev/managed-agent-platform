- **Vault `environment_variable` credentials now bind stored secrets to their approved destinations** ([#1052](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/1052); [plan 70](docs/plan/70_security-hardening-vault-egress.md) slice 1) — Updating an `environment_variable` credential to expand its `auth.networking` policy (`limited` to `unrestricted`, or adding hosts not covered by the stored `allowed_hosts`) now requires supplying `secret_value` for all roles, returning `400 invalid_request_error` when omitted. Preserving or narrowing destinations, changing `injection_location`, and metadata-only or `display_name`-only updates continue to work without resending the secret. Each create and auth update seals a canonical destination digest (`approved_networking_sha256`) inside the encrypted secret map; `vaultresolve.Credentials` fails closed if the stored `auth.networking` column diverges from that digest, while pre-existing credentials lacking the digest remain readable until their next auth update.
