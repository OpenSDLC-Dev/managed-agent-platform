- **Only a worker's credential may post a `user.tool_result`** ([#662](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/662)) — **breaking for management-key clients.** `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/events` now decides who may post a `user.tool_result` — the result of a built-in toolset call — by the credential that signed the request, as the reference does, rather than by the session's environment kind. A management `x-api-key`, or a human signed in through the identity lane, posting one is refused 403 `permission_error` on any session, and a batch carrying one appends nothing. On a `self_hosted` session a management key used to be accepted, so it could answer a call the worker was to answer; on a `cloud` session the refusal was a 400. Nothing else a management caller posts changes: `user.custom_tool_result`, `user.tool_confirmation` and `user.interrupt` are admitted as before. The environment key and the per-item sessions token are still accepted, so the BYOC worker and `ant beta:worker` are unaffected. A client that posted `user.tool_result` events with a management key must send them with the environment's key instead.
