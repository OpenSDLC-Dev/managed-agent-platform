- **The deployment scheduler: a cron schedule now fires** (plan 37 slice 4, [#51](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/51)). A 30-second controlplane sweep — every replica runs it; at most one committed run per occurrence rests on the partial unique index over `(deployment_id, scheduled_at)`, the reference's own idempotency key, not leader election — reads one clock (`SELECT now()`, Postgres's) and fires each due deployment's single most recent occurrence inside a one-hour catch-up window: "missed triggers are not backfilled", generalized from unpause to downtime. The fire is the manual trigger's transaction with the schedule's two differences: a classified failure auto-pauses the deployment (`paused_reason.error` carries the type; the fourteen-member union is asserted against the migration's CHECK), and an unclassified one rolls back whole — no run row, no pause, the next tick retries — because `unknown_error` would auto-pause with no auto-resume shipped. The fired session is unattributed (`created_by` NULL: a ticker has no principal). Archive, pause, unpause and the fire itself take a 5-second `lock_timeout`, so neither side of the row contention can hang the other. Observability: spans `deployment.tick`/`deployment.fire` (rooted — no inbound request) and three instruments — `deployment.fires` by outcome, `deployment.occurrences.skipped` counted by the claim's winner after commit, `deployment.tick.duration`. Twelve registry entries ([docs/DIVERGENCES.md](docs/DIVERGENCES.md)), the no-jitter and catch-up-window decisions among them.
