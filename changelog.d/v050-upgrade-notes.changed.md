- **Upgrading from v0.4.0 applies migrations 0041–0047 in one transaction, which holds each table lock it takes until it commits or rolls back** — The first v0.5.0 controlplane, brain or executor to start runs all seven, the migrations of #720, #663, #643, #685, #674, #578 and #856 in that order. It takes `sessions`, `session_threads`, `deployment_runs`, `work_session_tokens`, `files` and `deleted_sessions` ACCESS EXCLUSIVE, and while it holds them every read and write of those tables, from any replica, waits. The run's length grows with three pieces of work: 0041 validates its CHECK over every `session_threads` row, 0044 scans `memory_stores` to backfill archived stores' `updated_at`, and 0046 builds three indexes on `files`, measured under 0.4 seconds on a million rows. 0043, 0045, 0046 and 0047 give up a lock they cannot have within 2 seconds; 0041, 0042 and 0044 set no bound of their own, so the run can wait for theirs indefinitely unless the database, the role or the DSN sets a `lock_timeout` or `statement_timeout`. A give-up or a deadlock rolls the whole run back, releasing every lock, and starts it again after 1, 2, 4, then 8 seconds; a start whose fifth attempt fails exits with the error, and the next start tries again.
