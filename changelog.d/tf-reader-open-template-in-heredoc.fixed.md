- **The two `.tf` readers could drop a resource from a file Terraform accepts, silently** ([#762](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/762), from [#767](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/767)) — Terraform does not end a heredoc at a line reading the terminator when that line sits inside an open `${…}` or `%{…}`; it is expression text and the heredoc runs on. `deploy/gcp/check_split.py` and `tools/kmsrole/hcl.go` closed at the first such line, so the rest of the body arrived as configuration — enough to hide a resource outright, because the leaked body can open a heredoc only the readers see, swallowing the brace that closes the enclosing block and a whole `resource` with it, while a stray `{` left behind balances the count again. Both reported every other resource, and no error, over a file `terraform fmt` formats. Both now refuse a body line ending with a template still open. Deciding that means knowing where the template ends on its line, and in HCL three constructs make a brace something other than structure: a quoted string, walked to its close unless an interpolation opens inside it first (`${"${"}"}"` is one string, not two); a comment, which answers open outright when it runs to end of line; and a heredoc, needing no rule because its marker must end the line. Anything unanswerable answers open. An ordinary multi-line template in a heredoc body is refused with it, deliberately; `deploy/gcp/` holds none.
