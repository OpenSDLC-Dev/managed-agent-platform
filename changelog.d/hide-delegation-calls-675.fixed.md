- **Delegation calls no longer appear on any events list or stream** ([#675](https://github.com/OpenSDLC-Dev/managed-agent-platform/issues/675), part 1) — A coordinator's `create_agent`, `send_to_agent`, `list_agents` and `wait_for_agents`, and a child's `send_to_parent` and `submit_result`, were rendered as `agent.tool_use` with the `agent.tool_result` answering each: on the session view and the primary thread's, on the calling child's own, and — through the `self_hosted` widening — a child's on the session view too. The reference's recorded lists show neither half, only the `session.thread_created` and `agent.thread_message_sent`/`_received` events a delegation already writes, and neither is rendered now. Ordinary tool calls, cross-posted ones included, are unchanged, and a page still holds `limit` rows. The rows stay in the log, where a thread's replay reads them. The `coordinator-team` eval now counts a spawn by its `session.thread_created`.
