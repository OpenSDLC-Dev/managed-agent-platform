- **A terminal grading settlement no longer depends on when a child's ending notice landed (#801)** — After a `satisfied` or `failed` verdict with budget left, or a failed grader call, the coordinator now chains only on client input or on a message from a child that is still live, never on an ending notice. Before, an archive notice landing during the grader's call ran an extra coordinator turn, while the same notice landing a moment earlier, or a child's "ended without reporting" notice, did not. An unread notice keeps `processed_at: null` until the coordinator's next request reads it.
